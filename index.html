Angelfish

Graceful freshwater fish with triangular fins, often found in aquariums.

Barracuda

Fast predator with sharp teeth, known for its speed and hunting skills.

Catfish

Bottom-dwelling fish with whisker-like barbels, popular in freshwater habitats.

Dolphin

Intelligent marine mammal, famous for its playfulness and social behavior.

Eel

Long, snake-like fish, often hiding in rocks and coral reefs.

Flounder

Flat fish that camouflages itself on the sea floor.

Grouper

Large reef fish, strong predator living in coral reefs.

Hammerhead Shark

Shark with a unique hammer-shaped head for better vision.

Jellyfish

Soft-bodied marine animal with tentacles, moves by pulsing.

Lionfish

Brightly colored fish with venomous spines, native to Indo-Pacific.

Manta Ray

Huge graceful ray, filter feeder, often seen jumping out of water.

Nurse Shark

Bottom-dwelling shark, usually harmless to humans.

Octopus

Highly intelligent cephalopod with eight arms.

Parrotfish

Colorful reef fish with beak-like teeth for eating algae.

Quillfish

Spiny fish that inflates its body when threatened, similar to pufferfish.

Ray

Flat-bodied fish with long tail, often glides gracefully over sea beds.

Seahorse

Small fish with horse-like head, males carry the babies.

Tuna

Fast-swimming ocean fish, important in commercial fishing.

Urchin

Spiny marine animal, moves slowly using tube feet, grazes on algae.

Viperfish

Deep-sea predator with huge teeth and bioluminescent lure.

Whale Shark

Largest fish in the world, gentle giant feeding on plankton.

X-ray Tetra

Transparent small freshwater fish, popular in aquariums.

Yellow Tang

Bright yellow reef fish, often kept in saltwater aquariums.

Zebra Shark

Spotted bottom-dwelling shark, also known as leopard shark.
